Today’s loans are rigid, and rigidity costs us

Each product has its own journey. Approved credit can’t be reused. On the website, due dates and terms are locked until the loan is repaid, and paying early means closing the whole loan.

Payday doesn’t match the due date
Customer misses a payment
Collections steps in
Trust erodes, customer leaves

This is our working hypothesis. Phase 1 starts by measuring it.

We’re behind on some things. On one thing, nobody is there yet.

We reviewed Maya, GCash, SeaBank/SPayLater, CIMB, Home Credit, BillEase, Tala and the large banks.

Catch up

Credit lines for repeat borrowing are everywhere. Home Credit already offers auto-debit from other banks, with a rebate for signing up.

Stay level

Partial prepayment, cashback for good behavior and twice-monthly schedules exist, mostly as promos or through customer service.

Be first

We found no lender letting customers extend a due date, change it after booking, or modify a loan themselves, in the app, on demand.

Three bets

One front door, a healthier book, and more from customers who already trust us.

Engage

One lending experience. One offer and one onboarding across products, with offers customers can shape. Fixes the leakiest part of the funnel.

Protect

Flexibility that keeps customers current. Extend, prepay, change, split and be nudged before a miss, all in the app.

Grow

Earn more from trust. Repeat borrowing, value-added services, new segments and merchant partners, without new acquisition cost.

How we’ll prove it

We don’t have financial baselines yet, so we aren’t publishing estimates. Every initiative has a metric to move, and Phase 1 starts by measuring the baseline.

1. Baseline

Measure today’s roll rates, collections effort, repeat borrowing and offer conversion before anything launches.

2. Pilot

Release each feature to a limited group and compare against customers without it.

3. Value it

Add financial value to each initiative only once real baselines and pilot results exist.

Risks we’ve already thought about

The first questions leadership will ask, and how we’ll answer them.

Does flexibility cost us income?We’ll compare any lost late-fee income against credit losses and collection effort.
Are extension fees fair?Fees are shown before confirming, and compliance reviews disclosure and restructuring rules.
Will it be abused?Eligibility rules and limits are part of each feature’s design.
Can we deliver?Auto-debit with other banks and merchant integration depend on partners, so discovery starts first.
Is Q4 2026 realistic?Five initiatives target it. We’ll confirm dates only once squads are named.
Does it protect what we have?We charge no pre-termination fee, unlike some lenders. We keep that edge.
