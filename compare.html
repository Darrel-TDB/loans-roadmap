What customers get today, and what changes

Both live loan offers on tonikbank.com, compared capability by capability with the roadmap proposal.

Our two offers today

Both are unsecured, fully in-app, and capped at ₱50,000. Source: tonikbank.com loan pages.

Credit Builder Loan

Apply in the Tonik app
  • Up to ₱50,000 over 6, 9 or 12 months
  • As low as 4.20% monthly add-on rate
  • No credit check or collateral; 1 valid ID and a Tonik Account
  • Age 23 to 58; instant approval, real-time disbursement
  • Early settlement free, by closing the whole loan
  • Due date and terms can’t be changed until fully repaid

Shop Installment Loan

Apply in-store at partner retailers
  • ₱5,000 to ₱50,000 over 3, 6, 9 or 12 months
  • Fixed 4.5% monthly interest (54% APR); no processing fee
  • Minimum 10% downpayment; up to 3 items per loan
  • Store promoter gives a QR code; confirmation code ends the sale
  • Early payment is free
  • Payments auto-debit from the Tonik Account at 10 PM on the due date

Both offers add the optional PayHinga insurance, which unlocks a payment holiday after 3 consecutive on-time payments. Repayment is by Pay Now (appears 7 days before the due date) or top-up via PESONet, InstaPay, over-the-counter or ECPay/GCash.
