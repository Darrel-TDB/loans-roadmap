Three things to take from this

One real gap

On-demand due-date extension, post-booking due-date change and self-serve modification are not offered anywhere we looked. This is where Tonik can lead.

Two gaps we must close

Reusable credit lines and auto-debit from other banks are already standard. They defend, rather than differentiate.

One edge to protect

Tonik charges no pre-termination fee. CIMB’s personal loan charges 3% of the unbilled amount, minimum ₱300.
