Every self-service feature removes a support call, and a reason to miss a payment. Try each journey as a customer would.

Today, customers are stuck. Proposed, they’re in control.

On tonikbank.com today, due dates and terms are locked until the loan is repaid, and early payment means closing the whole loan.

Today

  • Can’t change the due date until fully repaid
  • Can’t modify loan terms after approval
  • Paying early means settling the full balance
  • One monthly installment, whatever the payday

Proposed, all in-app

  • Extend a due date up to 15 days in two taps
  • Prepay any amount and see the balance update instantly
  • Split a bill into two payments at 0% interest
  • Reshape an offer before accepting it
